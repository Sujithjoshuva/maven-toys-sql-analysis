## Business Insights

- Art & Crafts ranked first in total units sold.
- Toys generated the highest total revenue and total profit.
- Electronics generated the highest profit margin at 44.85%, despite ranking fifth in units sold.
- Games ranked second in profit margin, showing stronger profitability efficiency than its revenue and total profit rankings.
- Sports & Outdoors ranked lowest in both total revenue and total profit among the five categories.
- The analysis demonstrates that sales volume, revenue, total profit, and profit margin can produce different performance rankings.
